Roadmap to the platinum

Play in this order. Stage 1 is the story; the rest is best done after the credits, when the whole map is open. The platinum lands with 100% Completion.

Missable trophies

Seven trophies need the right moment. A missable here means the trophy's moment can pass — a partner scenario, a one-time stunt, or a condition you must meet on the attempt. Story chapters and missions are not missable: missions can be replayed, so their trophies pop whenever you finish them.
